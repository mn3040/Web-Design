Pitching Revolution: Max Fried Anchors Yankees' Dominant Staff

May 6, 2025

Since joining the Yankees, Max Fried has been a game-changer. With a 6-0 record and a 1.01 ERA, he's provided stability to a rotation plagued by injuries. The team remains undefeated in his starts, underscoring his impact.

Key Performance Indicators

  • Team ERA: 3.12 (2nd in MLB)
  • Team K/9: 10.8 (1st in AL)
  • Team WHIP: 1.08 (1st in MLB)
  • First-pitch strike rate: 72% (1st in MLB)

The Yankees have embraced advanced analytics, optimizing pitch sequencing and leveraging real-time data to enhance performance. Fried's leadership and the staff's adaptability have been pivotal in this transformation.

19% Decrease in hard contact

Power and Patience: The Yankees' Offensive Philosophy

May 6, 2025

The Yankees' lineup balances traditional power with modern plate discipline. This approach has yielded impressive results in the 2025 season.

Offensive Metrics

  • Runs per game: 5.8 (1st in AL)
  • Team OBP: .352 (2nd in MLB)
  • Home runs: 89 (1st in MLB)
  • Walk rate: 11.2% (3rd in MLB)

Emphasizing both power and on-base skills, the Yankees have crafted a lineup capable of challenging any pitching staff.

42% Hard-hit ball rate
18% Chase rate (lowest in MLB)

Source: MLB.com
